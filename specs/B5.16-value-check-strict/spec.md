# B5.16 — `value_check` strict redesign

**Wave 5 (v0.4.0). Depends on:** B5.13 (SEV-01, `value_check` as shipped to v0.3.0's development line). **Asked by:** fraud-review-bedrock (its D-38, D-42). Decisions: D-72 (three outcomes), D-74 (OWNER: strict redesign, value_check leaves v0.3.0).

## Capabilities
- **SEV-01** (value-check half, redesigned): a deterministic check of the numbers in a decision's reasons against the record it was made on. `verified` only when every number-bearing statement in the reason is bound to a field by positive evidence and holds; anything else that carries a number is `unverifiable`; `contradicted` only when a bound statement clearly fails [D-65, D-72, D-74]. `render_evidence` and the faithfulness half of SEV-01 are unchanged.

## Goal
A verified share that can be published: no false claim is ever `verified`, so the share is a lower bound a reader can trust, even when it is lower than B5.13's.

## Why
B5.13's checker matched a number to whichever field was close in value when it could not tell which field a number was about. Seven verifier passes each closed the reported cases, and a fresh generator then found new ones (about 6,000 of 56,000 generated false claims came back `verified`). D-74 replaces "find the most plausible reading" with "accept only readings proven by the text's shape".

## Interface (exported from `evalkit.scorers` by the lead at merge, v0.4.0; this lane does not touch `__init__.py`)
```python
value_check(reason, record, *, phrases=(), tolerance=0.0, aliases=None, units=None,
            constants=(), ignore=(), unreadable=()) -> ScoreResult
value_check_scorer(*, phrases=(), name="evidence_values", record_key="record", tolerance=0.0,
                   aliases=None, units=None, constants=(), ignore=(), unreadable=(),
                   version="2") -> Scorer
value_check_shares(results, *, alpha=0.05) -> ValueCheckShares    # unchanged (D-72)
ValuePhrase(pattern, field=None, holds=None)                        # unchanged
```
D-72 output shape kept: `details["outcome"]` in verified / contradicted / unverifiable; `value` 1.0 only when verified; every check has a `status`; the scorer's per-reason `reasons`, `counts`, `shares`; `value_check_shares` unchanged.

## The rule (D-74)
1. **Mentions.** A field is mentioned only by its whole record key or an explicit alias, matched case-insensitively with spaces and underscores interchangeable (`risk_score` = "risk score" = "RISK_SCORE" = "riskscore"). A mention may not sit inside a longer word, a hyphenated word or a plural. Partial names ("card history" for `card_history_n`), misspellings and other subjects are not mentions. Overlapping mentions: the longest wins; one phrase aliased to two fields names neither.
2. **Bindings.** A number is bound to a mentioned field only by one of these shapes, token by token, nothing else between:
   - `<field> <conn> <number><unit?>` with conn in: is, was, equals, of, at, is at, was at, stands at, stood at, =, :, totals, totaled, totalled;
   - `<field> (<number><unit?>)`;
   - `<field> <number><unit?>` (adjacent);
   - `<field> [is|was] <cmp> <number><unit?>`, cmp in: above, over, below, under, exceeds, exceeded, exceeding, more/greater/higher/less/fewer/lower than, at least, at most, at or above, at or below, greater/less than or equal to, >, <, >=, <=, ≥, ≤;
   - `[<cmp>] <number><unit?> <field>` (e.g. "40 earlier purchases" with that alias, "more than 40 earlier purchases");
   - a `ValuePhrase(field=...)` match holding exactly one number (that number is bound to the phrase's field; a comparison word directly before the number gives the comparison; a hedge, range or list mark right after it — or, to, -, +, /, plus — makes it unbound).
   - No postfix comparisons: "41 or more" leaves "more" as a comparison word no binding used, so the reason is unverifiable (fraud-review-bedrock D-42 is stricter here).
   The token before a left-hand mention must be the start, punctuation, a line-start bullet, or one of: and, but, while, whereas, with, so, because, since, as, when, where, at, then, also, which, that — optionally followed by a determiner (the, this, a, an). "risk of the amount", "the average amount", "We note amount" bind nothing (verify513f gen.py, pass 6). A mention right after another mention joined by and / or / & / / / , is coordinated: no binding. After the number (and unit), the next token must be the end, a line break, punctuation (`. , ; : ! ? ) ] ( — –`), or one of: and, but, while, whereas, so, which, is, was, are, were. For the number-then-field shape the same holds after the field, and the token before the number must be the start, punctuation, a comparison, or one of: has, had, have, with, and, but, only, is, was, are, were, the, so.
   A number is bound only when exactly one field (with one comparison) is bound to it; a number attached on both sides, or bound to two fields, is unbound. A mention attached to two numbers binds neither.
3. **Never by value.** No number is ever matched to a field because its value is close. Unbound numbers are `unbound` checks and make the reason unverifiable.
4. **Units.** Without a declared unit only a bare number binds. `units={field: kind}` declares: `fraction` (field holds 0–1; "83%" / "83 percent" read as 0.83), `percent` (field holds 0–100; "%" read as is), `multiple` ("9x", "9 times", "9-fold", "9 ×"), `currency` ("$812.40", "812.40 USD", "dollars"), `hour` (field holds 0–23; "3 a.m.", "3pm", "15:00"; minutes other than :00 are unbound). Scaling words and suffixes (k, m, bn, thousand, million, cents, ordinals, exponents) never bind. A phrase-bound number may carry a multiple or currency mark; "%" still needs a declared unit.
5. **Tolerance.** Integer fields (an `int` value) are exact: "card history is 44" on 40 is contradicted, with any tolerance. A non-integer field equals a claim when it rounds to the claim at the claim's displayed precision AND is within max(0.005, 0.5% of the value) (fraud-review-bedrock D-42) AND at most 10% off; or when it is within `tolerance` (relative, default 0, non-integer fields only). A correct but coarser rounding ("0.8" or "1" on 0.83, "9x" on 9.1) is ambiguous (`coarse_rounding`); one unit off in the last digit shown (a truncation, "18.21" on 18.215) is ambiguous (`rounding`) unless more than 10% off; anything else is a failure. Floats are compared at their shortest decimal repr (0.83 is 0.83). Comparisons are exact. On a `fraction` field a bare number above 1 is unchecked (`unit:<field>`).
6. **Statements that can never verify.** Approximate words (about, roughly, nearly, ~, close to, …) break the shapes. Lists with "respectively", ranges ("800-900", "between"), times without an hour field, number words (one … ninety, hundred, thousand, twice, half, dozen, midnight, noon), non-ASCII digits and fractions, malformed numbers ("1.2.3") are unbound. Ids glued to letters ("P7.1", "v2") are not numbers. Policy `constants` are only labelled (reason `constant`); they never bind. `ignore` regexes mark id spans: their numbers are not claims, but negations and mentions inside them are still read.
7. **Negation and hedges.** A negation (not, no, never, n't, none, neither, nor, without, cannot, …), a hypothetical, condition or time-shift word (if, unless, would, could, might, may, should, will, whether, expected, estimated, previously, formerly, prior, last, ago, and D-42's requires, needs, must, when, otherwise, fits, criteria, conditions, eligible, allows, permits, satisf*, qualif*), "respectively", a "?", a comparison word no binding used ("$812, or less than that", "under P6.1"), or a match of a caller's `unreadable` regex outside every phrase match, anywhere in the reason makes every check of the reason `ambiguous`: it neither verifies nor contradicts (reason-wide scope). A denial word ("false" not bound to a boolean field, untrue, incorrect, wrong, inaccurate, mistaken, …) does the same.
7a. **Clauses** (D-42). A clause runs between sentence ends, `, ; ( ) [ ] — –`, line breaks and the words and, but, while, whereas, so, which, yet, because, although, though, since. A clause whose checks bind two fields (`unclear_field`), that holds a rule id glued to letters (`rule_id`), or keeps a threshold, baseline or range word outside every phrase match (`block_word`: threshold, signal, limit, rule, clause, window, range, period, between, from, until, through, respectively, cutoff, ceiling, criteri*, policy, requirement, usual, typical, median, normal, average, mean, baseline, norm, trigger, bar, each, both, +) has its checks `ambiguous`.
8. **Booleans.** `<field> is|was|=|: true|false`, `<field> true|false` and `(<field> true)` are checked against a boolean field.
9. **Condition phrases.** `ValuePhrase(holds=...)` matches are checked with the RUL-01 predicate grammar, as before.
10. **Outcome.** `contradicted` when any check failed; `verified` when there is at least one check and every check is ok; else `unverifiable`. Statuses: ok, failed, unbound (a number not bound: reason code), ambiguous (bound but voided: negation, hypothetical, denial, clause flags, roundings), unchecked (missing value, wrong type, `unit:<field>`).
10a. **verify516 (gen3).** A phrase's number, or a right-hand field's ("[<cmp>] <n> <field>"), binds nothing when another field (or an alias of one) is named right before or after it or anywhere in its clause (mentions of the phrase's own field excepted). The right-hand shape follows only the start, a sentence end (`. ! ? ;`), a line break, a joining word (and, but, with, while, whereas, so, which), or "<subject> has|had|have" whose subject is a word of that field's own key or alias, itself at the start of its phrase. A hedge (roughly, about, approximately, approx, around, nearly, almost, ballpark, circa, `~`, `≈`, give or take, more or less, or so, or thereabouts) anywhere in the reason makes every check ambiguous (`hedged`). A "true"/"false" not bound to a boolean field is an unbound check (found by fraud-review's mutation test: "..., merchant_first_seen is true" after a comma read as coordination was dropped).
11. **fraud-review-bedrock D-42.** Its strict checker's mutation test (tests/test_valuecheck.py) is run against this one in the lane's scratch area with its vocabulary as config; where its rules are stricter (tolerance, postfix, clause rules, hypothetical words, unreadable merchant and rarity words, fraction above 1) this check adopts them.

## Out of scope
Arithmetic between fields, string fields, dates, ranges and windows as claims (always unbound), any reading by value.
